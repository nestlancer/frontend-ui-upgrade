01PRIVACY POLICY

On this page

Every section of this document, in order.

Sections marked “not in this version” are listed in the outline but not yet published in this document.

01

Who we are

Nestlancer Studio Private Limited ("Nestlancer", "we") is a product studio registered in Hyderabad, India. This policy covers nestlancer.com, app.nestlancer.com, and admin.nestlancer.com.

02

Data we collect

  • Account data — name, email, company, and credentials for client and operator accounts.
  • Project data — briefs, quotes, files, messages, and approvals you place in the portal.
  • Payment metadata — amounts, UTR references, and Razorpay order IDs. Card and UPI credentials are processed by Razorpay; we never see them.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, and device/browser signatures used for security sessions.
03

How we use it

Strictly to deliver contracted studio work: operating your Project Hub, issuing quotes and GST invoices, processing milestone payments, and preventing abuse. We do not sell personal data, and we do not run third-party ad trackers in the portal.

04

Payment data

Payments are processed by Razorpay under their PCI-DSS certification. Bank-transfer payments are reconciled against UTR references you submit; those references are stored as financial records for the period required by Indian tax law.

05

Your rights

You may export your account data (Settings → Account → Data export), correct it, or request deletion. Deletion schedules immediately and completes within 30 days unless an open payment or Indian retention law requires a longer hold.

06

Contact the DPO

privacy@nestlancer.com · Grievance officer response within 48 hours.
