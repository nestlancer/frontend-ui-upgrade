What we shipped

Unified payouts and reconciliation for a lending platform. Designed and shipped a payments hub that normalizes Razorpay webhooks, idempotent payouts, and daily reconciliation exports for finance ops.

Outcomes

  • Zero duplicate payouts under replayed webhooks
  • Finance-ready settlement CSV in under 5 minutes
  • Clear audit trail for every ledger movement

Settlement day went from a three-person spreadsheet recon to a queue where mismatches surface in four minutes with suggested resolutions. The CFO signs off day-end summaries from her phone.
