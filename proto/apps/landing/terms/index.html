1. Acceptance

By creating an account or signing a quote you accept these terms. Individual quotes may add project-specific terms; where they conflict, the quote wins.

2. Accounts

Client accounts are self-serve. Operator accounts are issued by Nestlancer and cannot be created from the public portal. You are responsible for credential hygiene; sessions are revocable under Settings → Security.

3. Quotes & payments

  • Quotes are fixed-price and valid for the period shown on them (typically 10 days).
  • Electronic acceptance signs the Nestlancer service agreement for that project.
  • Payments follow the milestone schedule in the quote; late payment may pause work.
  • Refunds for undelivered milestones are issued to the original rail within 7 business days.

4. Delivery & acceptance

Deliverables are accepted through the portal's approval gates. Silence for 10 business days after a delivery note counts as acceptance, unless the quote says otherwise.

5. Intellectual property

On full payment, final deliverables transfer to you. Nestlancer retains ownership of pre-existing tools, libraries, and generic components.

6. Confidentiality

Both parties keep non-public project information confidential for 3 years after project close.

7. Limitation of liability

Aggregate liability is limited to fees paid under the relevant quote, except where law forbids such limitation.

8. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India; courts at Hyderabad have exclusive jurisdiction unless otherwise agreed in writing.
