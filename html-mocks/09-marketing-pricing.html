Pricing FAQ

How studio packages, milestones, and payments work.

Is Nestlancer a subscription or marketplace?

Neither. Nestlancer is a single dedicated product studio. You pay fixed milestone installments for scoped software delivery — never a recurring monthly seat subscription or marketplace commission.

How do milestone payments work?

After you submit a request, the studio sends an itemized quote with a Deposit (30%), Mid-project (40%), and Final (30%) schedule. You fund each milestone securely via Razorpay or NEFT/UPI as work ships.

What is included in the ₹59,000 Web Application MVP?

Discovery workshop, UI/UX design for core workflows, Next.js + API implementation, QA testing, production deployment, and 2 revision rounds per milestone.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Nestlancer serves clients across India, the US, UK, UAE, and worldwide with compliant export invoicing and asynchronous Project Hub tracking.
