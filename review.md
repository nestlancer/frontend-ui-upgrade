# 🌐 Tools to Review & Grade Your Web Application UI + HTML

---

## 🏆 CATEGORY 1: Website Graders (Give Scores / Grades)

These tools analyze your HTML page and give you an **actual grade or score out of 100**.

---

### 1. 🥇 **HubSpot Website Grader** *(Most Popular)*
🔗 `website.grader.com`

HubSpot's Website Grader is a well-established tool that has grown to cover a variety of SEO areas. In one glance, it shows:
- Overall website performance (page speed, load times)
- Mobile compatibility
- Various SEO and security checks

✅ Free | ✅ No signup needed | ✅ Gives clear A-F style scoring

---

### 2. 🥈 **QuickSEO Website Grader**
🔗 `quickseo.ai/tools/website-grader`

It scans your page for common issues across multiple categories — SEO configuration, security headers, page performance, and accessibility — and produces a **score out of 100** along with specific recommendations. Unlike full-scale audits that can take hours, it gives you an instant snapshot of your site's health — the fastest way to identify quick wins that can improve your search rankings, user experience, and overall site quality.

You'll see your total score out of 100 with a **color-coded rating** (green for great, yellow for needs work, red for poor).

---

### 3. 🥉 **DareBoost**
🔗 `dareboost.com`

DareBoost is a powerful grader tool and offers one of the most in-depth 100% free reports (no email or free trial required). It provides an overall quality score, as well as scores for:
- Security
- Accessibility
- Compliance
- Quality
- SEO
- Browser rendering

---

### 4. 🏅 **Nibbler**
🔗 `nibbler.silktide.com`

Nibbler is a browser-based grading tool that focuses on general qualities of your site and offers actionable advice. It focuses on five major categories:
- **Overall**
- **Accessibility**
- **Experience**
- **Marketing**
- **Technology**

It also has an overview section that shows scores for popularity, code quality, content quantity, and more.

---

### 5. **WooRank**
🔗 `woorank.com`

WooRank provides a wealth of data about your website. It starts with an **overall grade of 1 to 100** and then goes into detail about SEO, Mobile, Usability, and Technologies. The tool includes a checklist that outlines what you should do to improve each of the important categories and also indicates the difficulty of each option.

---

### 6. **Roast My Web**
🔗 `roastmyweb.com`

The audit checks **UX, copy, on-page SEO, mobile layout, and conversion friction**. You get an editable report with category scores and a ranked fix list.

---

### 7. **Rise Marketing Website Grader**
🔗 `rise.co/tool/website-grader`

This free tool harnesses proprietary technology to analyze your website in under two minutes! It generates a report that scores your website on **usability, security, marketing, SEO and performance**.

---

## 🖼️ CATEGORY 2: UI Screenshot Review & Visual Annotation Tools

These tools let you **upload or capture screenshots** of your web UI and leave feedback/annotations.

---

### 8. 📸 **Capian** *(Best for UX Audits)*
🔗 `capian.co`

Capian is a UX audit and interface review tool for professionals. It lets you capture findings with a Chrome extension, organize with built-in heuristic criteria, and share your report instantly. Use the Chrome extension and mobile app to save screenshots and make annotations while you browse, then let Capian create your report automatically.

---

### 9. 🐞 **BugHerd** *(Best for Teams/Agencies)*
🔗 `bugherd.com`

BugHerd is the best website feedback software with visual annotation features because it lets users click directly on live web pages to leave annotated comments, automatically captures screenshots and technical metadata, and turns feedback into actionable tasks for your team.

---

### 10. 📌 **Marker.io**
🔗 `marker.io`

Reviewers click the affected area, add visual comments, and describe the issue. Marker.io captures **annotated screenshots, session replays, and technical details** – browser, OS, console logs, and screen size – automatically, so developers get a clear picture.

Reviewing your website isn't just about catching visual bugs. With Website Monitoring, Marker.io scans every page against **WCAG 2.2 AA** on a schedule, weekly or monthly, or on demand. Each accessibility issue comes with a screenshot of the failing element, technical details, and a plain-English guide to fixing it, from missing alt text to poor color contrast.

---

### 11. **Webvizio**
🔗 `webvizio.com`

Webvizio lets people **annotate and comment on live websites or designs** with visual pins and context.

---

## ⚙️ CATEGORY 3: HTML Code Review & Technical Validators

These tools look **inside your HTML code** and flag errors:

---

### 12. ✅ **W3C Validator**
🔗 `validator.w3.org`

The W3C Validator tool is an easy way to **identify errors and warnings related to your HTML code**.

---

### 13. 🚀 **Google PageSpeed Insights**
🔗 `pagespeed.web.dev`

Google PageSpeed Insights is a page speed grader that **scores your mobile and desktop sites** based on the number and severity of performance issues, including your Core Web Vitals.

---

### 14. 🕷️ **Screaming Frog SEO Spider**
🔗 `screamingfrog.co.uk`

Screaming Frog is primarily a crawler used for technical SEO audits. It doesn't show DA or DR on its own, but you can combine it with services like Moz's API or Ahrefs to get detailed checks. You get extensive site crawl data like H1 tags, meta descriptions, and status codes.

---

### 15. 🔍 **Applitools Eyes** *(AI-Powered Visual Review)*
🔗 `applitools.com`

Applitools Eyes is a visual testing platform that uses **AI-based visual comparison** to detect UI changes across browsers, devices, and screen sizes. Instead of relying purely on pixel-by-pixel screenshot comparisons, it uses a Visual AI engine that analyzes UI structure and layout to identify meaningful visual differences.
